About me

My name is Antonín Lamač, but in the digital and creative world you will find me under the pseudonym Tony Brenx. I am a photographer and cameraman working primarily in the Karlovy Vary Region (Sokolov, Karlovy Vary, Cheb), but I will travel anywhere for an interesting project. Raw black-and-white street photography is my heart's passion, but I do not only shoot and film in black and white – I also create fully colour commercial and creative projects. Whether it is authentic portraits, corporate events or dynamic video production with cinematic colour grading. You will often find me with a Lumix in hand, paired with old manual lenses.
